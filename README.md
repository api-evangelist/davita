# DaVita (davita)
DaVita Inc. is a Fortune 500 kidney care company operating in-center hemodialysis, home hemodialysis, and peritoneal dialysis programs in the United States and abroad, alongside transplant support, integrated kidney care, hospital partnerships, and clinical research. DaVita does not publish a public developer API; integration with the company is handled through patient-facing portals, payer and provider partnerships, and electronic health record data exchange standards.
